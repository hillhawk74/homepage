Course Description and Meeting Times

Math 723, Differential Equations I, Cr. 3.
Description: This course is to teach students some important concepts, formulas and theorems in partial differential equations.
Prerequisite: Math 703, 704 or permission of instructor
Times and Place: MW 3:55pm-5:10pm, LeConte College 401

Textbook

TEXTBOOK: Partial Differential Equations, by Lawrence C. Evans, 2nd Edition, American Mathematical Society, 2010.

Learning Outcomes

Students are expected to master principal theories and concepts to identify those important differential equations for which explicit formulas can be had for solutions, and at the same time apply the methodology learned from this course to find direct formulas or far less concrete representation formulas for certain type of partial differential equations.

Reading

Reading the textbook in advance of the lecture is strongly encouraged. Benefits of this preparation include obtaining a familiarity with the terminology and concepts that will be encountered (so you can distinguish major points from side issues), being able to formulate questions about the parts of the presentation that you do not understand, and having a chance to review the skills and techniques that will be needed to apply the new concepts and representation formulas for certain type of partial differential equations.

Course Outline

We cover the materials presented in Chapters 1, 2 and 3 of the textbook.
