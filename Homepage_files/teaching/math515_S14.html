Course Description and Meeting Times

Math 515 (Stat 523), Financial Mathematics II, Cr. 3.
Prerequisite: A grade of C or better in Math 514.
Lectures: TTh 2:50pm-4:05am, LeConte College 405

Learning Outcomes

The primary goal of this course is to teach students some necessary mathematical techniques and how to apply them to the fundamental concepts and problems in financial mathematics and their analytic and numerical solution.

Textbook and Course Outline

Reference Books:
  • Elementary Stochastic Calculus with Finance in View, by Thomas Mikosch, World Scientific, 1998.
  • Elementary Calculus of Financial Mathematics, by A. J. Roberts, SIAM, 2009.
Tentatively Covered Contents
Lectures Times Content
Weeks 1-3 Basic probability theory
Weeks 4-6 Brownian motion and stochastic differential equation
Weeks 7-10 Basic stochastic calculus
Weeks 11-13 Stochastic integral and Ito's formula
Weeks 14-15 Option pricing and Black-Scholes equation

Grading Policy

Course grades will be determined from student performance on exams and homework. There will be three exams: two 50 minute in-class exams and a final exam. The two in-class exams, which are indicated on the syllabus above, will be given during the time normally used for lecture. Each of these exams will test only the material covered since the previous exam. In contrast the final exam, which will be a take-home cummulative exam given during the week of final exams. Reason for missing an exam must be properly documented and any missed exam must be made up within a week. Homework will be assigned for each covered section.
