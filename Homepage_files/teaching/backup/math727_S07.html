Course Description and Meeting Times

Math 727, Numerical Analysis II , Cr. 3.
Description: This course is a continuation of the course Math 726 and is to teach students some basic and important concepts and algorithms in numerical analysis including the following topics: boundary value problems for ODEs; approximation of eigenvalue problem; solution of linear systems; solution of nonlinear equations and numerical optimization. Pactice with programming is also emphasized.
Prerequisite: Math 726
Times and Place: MWF 1:25pm-2:15pm, LeConte College 121


Textbook and Course Outline

TEXTBOOK : Numerical Mathematics, by Alfio Quarteroni, Riccardo Sacco and Fausto Saleri, Springer, 2000.
REFERENCES:
  • Concise Numerical Mathematics, By Robert Plato, AMS, 2003.
  • Iterative Methods for Linear and Nonlinear Equations, by C.T. Kelley, SIAM, 1995.
  • An Introduction to Numerical Analysis, By Endre Suli and David F. Mayers, Cambridge University Press, 2003.
Tentative Schedule and Lecture Contents
Weeks Content
Weeks 1-2 Boundary value problems for ODEs
Weeks 3-5 Approximation of eigenvalue problems
Weeks 6-8 Basic iterative methods for solving linear systems
Weeks 9-11 CG method and GMRES method
Weeks 12-13 Root finding for nonlinear equations
Weeks 13-14 Solution of systems of nolinear equations
Weeks 14-16 Unconstrained optimization
