Course Description and Meeting Times

Math 728J, Scientific Computing I, Cr. 3.
Description: This course is to teach students the mathematical basis of iterative methods for linear systems as well as the corresponding parallel computing methodology. Practice with sequential and parallel programming is also emphasized.
Prerequisite: Some knowledge of numerical linear algebra and prior programming experience.
Times: MWF 10:10-11:00am, LeConte College 316

Textbook and Course Outline

TEXTBOOK : Iterative Methods for Sparse Linear systems, 2nd Edition , by Y. Saad, SIAM, 2003.
REFERENCES:
  • Iterative Methods for Linear and Nonlinear Equations, by C.T. Kelley, SIAM, 1995.
  • Matrix Computations, 3rd Edition, by G.H. Golub and C.F. Van Loan, Johns Hopkins University Press, 1996.
  • Parallel Programming with MPI, 1st Edition, by P. Pacheco, Morgan Kaufmann, 1996.
  • MPI Standard.
Tentative Schedule and Lecture Contents
Weeks Content
Weeks 1-3 Basic Concepts and Stationary Iterative Methods
Weeks 4-5 Conjugate Gradient (CG) Method (theory, algorithm and preconditioning)
Weeks 6 Projection Methods
Weeks 7-9 Generalized Minimum RESidual (GMRES) Method (theory, algorithm and preconditioning)
Weeks 10-11 Bi-CG, QMR, CGS and Bi-CGSTAB methods
Weeks 12-15 Messgae Passing Interface (basic communication subroutines)
Weeks 16 Parall Solver of Linear Systems
