Course Description and Meeting Times

Math 527, Numerical Analysis, Cr. 3.
Prerequisite: A grade C or better in Math 242 or 520
Lectures: MWF 10:10am-11:00am, LeConte College 115

Textbook

TEXTBOOK: Numerical Mathematics and Computing (6th ed.), by W. Cheney and D. Kincaid, Thomson Brooks/Cole, 2008

Learning Outcomes

Students will master basic concepts and algorithms to solve problems related to root finding, interpolation and approximation of functions, numerical differentiation and integration, least squares fitting, numerical solutions of ordinary differential equations, Monte Carlo Methods, and etc..

Grading Policy

Course grades will be determined from student performance on exams and homework. There will be three exams: two 50 minute in-class exams and a final exam/project. The two in-class exams, which are indicated on the syllabus above, will be given during the time normally used for lecture. Each of these exams will test only the material covered since the previous exam. In contrast the final exam/project, which will be given during the week of final exams, will be a cummulative exam. Reason for missing an exam must be properly documented and any missed exam must be made up within a week. Homework will be assigned for each covered section. The grades for the course are determined as follows:

 Exam 1  25%  Exam 2  25%  Final Exam/Project  25%  Homework  25% ----  Total  100%

86-100: A 81-85: B+ 76-80: B 71-75: C+ 66-70: C 60-65: D 0-59: F

Attendance and Academic Honesty

Attendance at every class meeting is important and expected. Students missing more than 10% of the class meetings (4 days) can have their grade lowered. Cheating and plagiarism will not be tolerated. Violations of this policy will be dealt with according to University guidelines.
